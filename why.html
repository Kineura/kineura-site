Why Kineura.

We want people to be healthier and to perform better. Everything we build, and everything we do with your data, comes back to that.

Why we exist

Kineura exists to help you keep track of how you train and how you feel, in one place, with as little effort as possible, and to help you plan what comes next.

Why data and science are at the centre

We're a science-based company. We'd rather tell you something true and modest than something impressive and wrong, and we say plainly what our measurements can and can't tell you.

Doing that well takes real-world data.

How your data makes Kineura better

When you use Kineura, your data, with your name, email and account details removed, helps us find where Kineura gets things wrong and fix it.

So the data works for you twice: once directly, in your own log, and again as the product improves for you and for everyone who trains with it. That's the whole idea. Better tools, built from real training, for the people doing the training.

If you want to go further, you can choose to include the biometric and activity data from your connected wearables and health apps too (where the service it comes from allows it), and to let us use your de-identified data in future research. That's optional, it's off unless you turn it on, and if we ever publish research or work with a research partner we'll ask you again, separately, with the full picture.

What we promise

  • We never sell your data. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone.
  • No ads. We don't make money from your attention, and we never use your data for advertising.
  • We will never try to identify you from de-identified data.
  • Your data is never used against you. We use it to give you a better product and to make Kineura better. That's it.
  • You can leave with your data deleted. Delete your account in the app at any time and your data goes with it.

We're doing everything we can to build the best product for you. Being on your side about your data is part of that, not separate from it.

The details are in our Privacy Policy, written in the same plain words.

Insights at every stride.
