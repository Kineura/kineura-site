Why Kineura.

We want people to be healthier and to perform better. Everything we build, and everything we do with your data, comes back to that.

Our vision

We believe sport, movement and play are central to a long, healthy life. And we believe that making the right choice easy, every day, leads to more progress, better health and more fun.

That means lowering the barrier to training: not just the best plan for a perfect day, but the best thing you can do in the twenty minutes you have, with whatever you have around you. The best plan should be as accessible as possible.

This is a belief, not a proven fact. The way we pursue it is scientific: we test what we do, measure the results, and say honestly what we find. Whether the vision holds is something we'll find out.

How training works

Training is applying a stimulus to your body in the hope that it adapts the way you want: stronger, faster, more durable, more skilled.

It's always a guess

Which stimulus, how much, how often, when to push and when to back off: every one of those is a guess. Some guesses are much better than others, but nobody knows exactly what your body needs. Anyone who claims certainty is overselling.

Why we exist

We exist to make it as easy as possible to do the best thing for you: your goals, your body, your constraints.

Why that takes data

A better guess needs to know what you did and how your body responded. That means what you trained, how it felt, and what your sensors and devices recorded. That's what Kineura collects, and only to serve this.

Why science

Science is how guesses get better: test them, measure the results, and be honest about how sure you are. We'd rather tell you something true and modest than something impressive and wrong. When we show you something, we'll say what it can and can't tell you.

How your data helps

Your data works for you first, in your own training. With your name, email and account details removed, it also helps us find where Kineura gets things wrong and fix it, for you and for everyone who uses it.

If you choose, you can also include data from your connected wearables and health apps, where the service it comes from allows it, and let us use your de-identified data in future research. It's off unless you turn it on. If we ever publish research or work with a research partner, we'll ask you again first.

What we promise

  • We never sell your data.
  • No ads, and your data is never used for advertising.
  • We'll never try to identify you from de-identified data.
  • Your data is never used against you.
  • Delete your account at any time, and your data goes with it.

The details are in our Privacy Policy.
