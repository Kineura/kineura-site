Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, who helps us handle it, and how you delete it. Written to be read, not skimmed past.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

In short

  • We're on your side. We use your data to make Kineura better for you, never against you.
  • We never sell your data. We never show ads.
  • With your name, email and account details removed, your data helps us test and improve Kineura. That stays inside Kineura, and we will never try to identify you.
  • AI service providers that work only for us read what you log, so you can log by just saying what you did. They don't train their models on your data. AI Transparency Statement.
  • Biometric and activity data from the wearables and health apps you connect (heart rate, sleep, workouts and the like) is there to help you. It's left out of our improvement work unless you choose to include it, and only where the service it comes from allows it.
  • You can see, correct, export and delete your data. Delete your account and its data at any time, in the app: Profile → Delete account.
  • Kineura is not a medical device, and nothing in it is medical advice.
  • Kineura is for people aged 16 and over.

Who we are

Kineura is made by KINEURA, LLC ("Kineura", "we", "us"). This policy covers the Kineura app and this website. If you have a question about it, or want to use any of the choices below, contact us.

Why we collect anything at all

See Why Kineura: what we're trying to do, and why it takes data.

What we collect

From you, your Kineura sensors, and the services you choose to connect:

  • Account details: your name, email, date of birth and gender.
  • Health and fitness data: your workouts and activity, and how your body feels. Plus what your sensors and connected wearables record, such as heart rate, sleep and other biometrics.
  • Location: a workout's route, only if a connected device records one.
  • What you write or say: notes and feedback. Spoken notes are turned into text, and the audio isn't kept.
  • Technical data: which version of the app you use. Diagnostic logs only if you choose to send them.

What each connected service shares is listed under Data from other services.

How we use it

  • To give you Kineura: keep your training log, show your own data back to you, and help you plan.
  • To improve Kineura, as described in the next section.
  • To keep it working and safe: fix problems, prevent abuse, and keep your account secure.
  • To contact you about your account, this policy, or the app.

How your data helps improve Kineura

This part is required to use the app, so here it is plainly. To make Kineura more accurate and more useful, the Kineura team reviews and analyses de-identified data from the app to check how accurately Kineura works, and uses what it learns to test and improve the app and its models.

  • De-identified first. Before the team works with it, your name, email and account details are removed. What's left is linked only to a random account ID, so that it can still be deleted when you delete your account.
  • "De-identified", not "anonymous". We use the honest word. Notes are free text, and if you mention your coach's name or your hometown in a note, that text is still there. We will never try to identify you from it.
  • Internal only. This work stays inside Kineura. We don't publish it as studies and we don't share it with research partners.
  • Not included: data from the wearables and health apps you connect, unless you opt in (below) and the service it comes from allows it.

Optional: help make training better for everyone

You can choose to go a step further. If you tick the optional box when you agree to this policy, or turn it on later in the app, you allow us to:

  • include your de-identified biometric and activity data from connected wearables and health apps in the improvement work described above, where the service it comes from allows it; and
  • include your de-identified data, including that biometric and activity data where allowed, in future research.

We don't run any research outside Kineura today. If we ever publish research or work with a research partner, we'll ask you separately first, with full information about what it is, before any of your data is part of it. Saying yes here doesn't sign you up for that.

Saying no changes nothing about how the app works for you. You can change your answer at any time in the app (Profile → Help make training better). If you turn it off, we stop including your data from then on. What we already learned from it, such as an improved model, can't be unpicked.

Data from other services

Kineura can bring in data from wearables, health apps and other services you connect. The same rules apply to all of them:

  • It's there to help you. We use it only to show you your own data in the app and to help you with your training.
  • It's kept separate and labelled. In the app, data from another service is shown as coming from that service.
  • It's left out of our improvement work, research and model training, unless the service allows it and you choose to include it through the optional choice above.
  • Each service's own rules come first. Where a service sets stricter terms on how its data may be used, stored or deleted, we follow them. Those rules, and how to disconnect each service, are listed below.
  • Disconnecting stops it. You can disconnect a service at any time in the app.
  • It's never sold, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with anyone except the service providers that run Kineura for us (see Who else handles your data).

Service by service

Apple Health

Kineura reads (and never writes) workouts, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, sleep stages, wrist temperature, steps and active energy. Kineura stores what it reads in your Kineura account. Apple Health data is never used for advertising, and is included in improvement work or research only if you opt in. To stop it, go to Profile → Connected sources → Disconnect & delete in the app. That stops Kineura reading Apple Health and deletes everything Kineura imported from it, on your phone and in your Kineura account. You can also turn off Kineura's access in your iPhone's Settings → Health.

Strava

Kineura reads your Strava activities, including their details where Strava has them (such as route, heart rate, cadence, power and laps). Strava data is never included in improvement work, research or model training, even if you opt in. Kineura doesn't post anything to your Strava account. To stop it, go to Profile → Connect Strava and disconnect, or remove Kineura in your Strava settings. Disconnecting revokes our access and deletes our connection to your Strava account. Activities already brought into Kineura stay in your account until you delete them or delete your account; if you'd like them deleted when you disconnect, contact us and we'll do it.

AI Transparency Statement

Kineura uses AI to understand what you tell it. Here is what that means for your data.

What AI does, and why

AI reads what you log, together with related training and connected-device data, so it can keep your training log accurate and help you plan. It's why you can log in one sentence instead of tapping through forms. Nothing it produces is medical advice, and you can always see and correct what it made.

Who does it

AI processing is done by AI service providers working on Kineura's behalf, under contract, only to provide Kineura to you (they're listed under Service providers). We only use providers whose terms don't allow them to train their models on your data; if that ever had to change, we'd ask you first. Your name and email aren't sent to them. Data from a connected service is processed by AI only where that service allows it.

Your choice

AI processing is part of what you agree to when you start using Kineura, because the app is built around it: without it, Kineura can't read your notes. You can withdraw that agreement at any time by deleting your account (Profile → Delete account), which deletes your data. If you'd like us to stop AI processing of your data without deleting your account, contact us; we'll stop it, though Kineura won't be able to read your notes from then on.

Who else handles your data

Only the service providers that run Kineura for us (listed below), the wearables and health apps you connect as the places your data comes from, and anyone the law requires us to disclose data to, for example in response to a valid legal request.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data.
  • We never show ads, and we never use your data for advertising.
  • We never use your data against you.
  • We never try to identify you from de-identified data.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your data until you delete your account. You can do that at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. It's permanent and can't be undone.

Deleting your account removes your sign-in and everything stored in your Kineura account: everything you logged, your sensor recordings and the results calculated from them, everything brought in from the wearables and health apps you connected, and any de-identified copies kept for improvement work. Where a connected service lets us, we also revoke Kineura's access to your account there. Deletion finishes on its own, usually within about an hour. Your phone also forgets the account.

A few things to know:

  • Backup copies and system logs expire within 90 days.
  • Note text already sent to an AI provider is kept, or not, under that provider's own retention rules.
  • A recording that is still on a Kineura sleeve and was never uploaded stays on the sleeve.
  • What we already learned from de-identified data, such as an improved model, can't be unpicked, but the data itself is deleted.

Your rights and how to use them

Wherever you live, you can do all of the following. Where the app can't do it yet, email us (see Contact) and we'll answer within 30 days.

  • Access. Most of your data is right there in the app. For a copy of everything we hold about you, email us.
  • Correction. Correct it in the app. For your account details, email us.
  • Deletion. Profile → Delete account, any time. To delete only some of your data, delete it in the app or email us.
  • Export (portability). Email us and we'll send you your data in a common, machine-readable format.
  • Objection. You can object to any use of your data, including our improvement work and AI processing. Email us and tell us what you object to.
  • Restriction. You can ask us to stop using some of your data while a question about it is sorted out. Email us.
  • The optional choice. Change it any time: Profile → Help make training better.
  • Disconnect other services any time (see Data from other services).
  • Withdraw your agreement to this policy by deleting your account. Kineura can't run without the required part.

Depending on where you live, the law may give you further rights, or a right to complain to a data protection authority. Ask and we'll help.

Security

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited to what each part of Kineura needs, and each person's data is kept to their own account. No system is perfectly secure, and we won't pretend otherwise.

If a security breach affects your data, we'll tell you without undue delay, and we'll tell any partner or authority we're required to tell.

Age

Kineura is for people aged 16 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe someone under 16 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

This website and its forms

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking. The home page plays a video hosted by Wix, so your browser connects to Wix's servers to load it.

If you fill in one of our forms (early access, coaches and teams, or research and clinical), we receive what you enter: your name, email address and message, and, on the collaboration forms, your organisation and the kind of collaboration you're interested in. The forms are sent through FormSubmit, a form-relay service, which delivers them to the Kineura team by email. FormSubmit shows a spam check (Google reCAPTCHA) when you submit. We use what you send only to reply to you and to follow up about Kineura. We keep it as long as we need it for that, and you can ask us to delete it at any time by contacting us.

Other companies' names

Apple, Apple Health, Strava, Anthropic, Amazon Web Services, Google, Wix and FormSubmit are names and trademarks of their owners. We mention them only to tell you how your data is handled; it doesn't mean they endorse Kineura.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll tell you in the app. If a change affects what you've agreed to, the app will ask you to agree again before you carry on. The date at the top always shows the latest version.

Service providers

These companies process data on Kineura's behalf, under contract, only to run Kineura. Each of them is bound to protect your data at least as well as this policy does.

  • Hosting, sign-in and storage: Amazon Web Services. Your data is stored in the United States.
  • AI service providers: Anthropic and Amazon Web Services. We may add Google; if we do, we'll update this list first.
  • Website forms: FormSubmit (see This website and its forms).
