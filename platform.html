A connected system, built in four parts.

Kineura is not one sensor. It's a system that measures muscle and motion, records it into a training timeline, analyzes it into metrics, and gets it in front of the people who act on it: the athlete, the coach, the clinician.

Measure

Available now

Wearable IMU sensing, and hybrid EMG + IMU sensing for muscle activation alongside motion. This is the part of the system with real, shipping hardware today.

  • Hardware: a lower-leg compression sleeve, IMU-only or EMG + IMU
  • Output: synchronized muscle-activation and motion signal, hundreds of samples per second

The current EMG + IMU sleeve.

Record

In development

A synchronized timeline that brings together wearable data, planned workouts, manual notes, and connected training context such as Strava, into one coherent record instead of scattered exports.

  • Job to be done: stop re-entering the same workout across three apps
  • Status: architecture defined, not yet shipping to early-access members
Read about the timeline

Concept imagery. The timeline product is not yet built.

Analyze

In development

Activity recognition, walk-run interval data collection, and video-based movement tracking for jump and sprint mechanics, turning raw signal and footage into metrics worth acting on.

  • Job to be done: answer "what actually changed in my mechanics," not just "what did I do"
  • Status: early prototyping, not yet available for pilots
Read about video analysis

Concept imagery. Video motion analysis is not yet built.

Act

In development

Review workflows built for how athletes, coaches, and clinical collaborators actually use movement data. A coach and a clinician need different views of the same session, not one dashboard trying to serve everyone the same way.

  • For coaches: roster-level review, not one athlete at a time
  • For clinicians: return-to-run progress tracking over a timeline
